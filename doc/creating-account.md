# Creating a Facebook Account

To create a Facebook account, go to facebook.com and click "Create New Account." Enter your name, email or phone number, password, and date of birth. Verify your account via email or SMS, and you're ready to start connecting.

## Steps
1. Visit facebook.com
2. Fill in the registration form
3. Verify your email or phone
4. Set up your profile

Remember to use a strong password and enable two-factor authentication for security.
