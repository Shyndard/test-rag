# Getting Started with Facebook

Facebook is a social networking platform that allows users to connect with friends, family, and communities worldwide. To get started, create an account, verify your email, and begin exploring the News Feed, where you can see updates from your connections.

## Key Features
- **News Feed**: View posts from friends and pages you follow.
- **Profile**: Customize your personal page with photos, bio, and interests.
- **Friends**: Send friend requests and build your network.

For more details, visit the Facebook Help Center.
